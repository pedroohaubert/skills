# Questions

Asks one business expert to decide or confirm what the work cannot settle alone. They read it once and answer in one pass.

1. **Recover the decisions.** From the plan, tickets, research, or code, separate facts with evidence, decisions already made, and points still open. Keep a point only when the answer changes something this person recognizes: a business rule, a formula, timing, a classification, or what a user sees or does. Code, files, tests, and implementation mechanics stay out. Done when every kept point would change an outcome they know.
2. **Open with one or two sentences:** what you are working on and what is already settled, so they do not decide it again.
3. **Write each question the way the user would ask it aloud**, in the words this person uses for the field, the report, and the process:
   - one rule per question, ordered by how much money or process it moves;
   - say what you understood, so "yes, that's it" is a complete answer, and name the other reading when one is still plausible;
   - put the number, formula, or a short real case inside the question when the rule is numeric or about dates;
   - end on the ask itself: the yes or no, the choice, or what to correct.
4. **Shape by count.** One or two questions go in paragraphs, each its own. Three or more are numbered, so the person can answer by number.

Done when each question can be answered with a confirmation, one of the options named, or a short correction, and every fact in it comes from the source or the user.

## Example

Request: confirm two rules for the late fee before finishing the calculation.

Ana, I am finishing the late fee calculation and two rules are still open. The rate and the grace period are already as you defined them.

On the start date: I understood the fee counts from the day after the due date, so an invoice due on the 10th starts on the 11th. Is that right, or does it start after the grace period ends?

On partial payments: today the fee is charged on the full invoice. Should it stay that way when part was already paid, or apply only to what is still open?

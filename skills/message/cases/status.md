# Status

Tells the reader which items were delivered, released, or moved to testing, so they know what to look at.

1. **Collect every item.** Take them from the commits, pull requests, and board for the period the user named, including work from outside the current conversation. Done when each item in that period is on your list or you can say why it is out.
2. **Open with one sentence:** what moved and where it is now ("These items are in the testing environment as of today").
3. **One bullet per item**, each a single line starting with a plain hyphen: the item's number and name, then what changes for the person using the system. Describe the behavior they will see, in their words.
4. **Close with what you need from them:** test, approve, answer by a date. Leave the closing out when nothing is needed.

Keep the list to items the reader can recognize or try. Internal work with no visible effect goes in one final bullet, or stays out.

## Example

These items are in the testing environment as of today:

- 21 (order total): the total now subtracts refunded items.
- 16 (export columns): the columns line up with the headers again.
- 34 (shipping estimate per region): the estimate now appears per region instead of as one number.

Could you test them by Friday? Whatever is approved ships on Monday.
